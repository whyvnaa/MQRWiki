    Quests and questlines¶

    The quest journal of every tribe, laid out like in the game. Story lines run left to right along the top; side, co-op and follow-up lines branch off below them. Drag the map to move around (scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to reset), pick a questline on the left to jump to it, and switch tribes with the tabs. Hover a quest for its level and XP, click it to open it. Title colours: story, primary, side quest, co-op. Dailies and events aren't in the journal; they are listed below.
