    Farming¶

    Farm in a group. Everyone gets the loot from enemies and chests, everyone gets kill XP, and playing close together gives a banana bonus.

    • Enemies drop something 25 % of the time, more often with more stars (loot).
    • Polished and Refined Shadow Shards only drop from spawners in high-level trails, from Forbidden Keep up.
    • Jack-in-the-boxes only work on highways, Crossroads Path and the event area.
    • An Elixir of Wealth gives +50 % bananas, but not on selling.
    • A knock-out costs some of the bananas you picked up since your last checkpoint.
