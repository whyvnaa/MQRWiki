    Builds¶

    Principles (all confirmed)¶

    • Damage per hit = base + weapon stat total. Base = 10 × level + the damage lines of your Ears, Wrist and Tail + skill points. The weapon is the biggest factor, then the three accessories.
    • Blunt counts for every element. You don't need matching elements. Pick the highest stat total. Tribe items are worth about 10 % more than Crossroads items of the same level, because they add an element line.
    • Item level beats rarity. Rarity adds only a few percent below Legendary. The level requirement doesn't affect stats.
    • Defence comes from Hat, Body, Legs and Backpack, plus skill points (which count against all elements). Enemies always deal a level-based minimum, and percentage hazards ignore defence.
    • Element defence: enemies attack with their tribe's element. Match your resist to the zone (for example Air resist against Chim Foo / mist enemies).
    • No set bonuses. Mix pieces freely.
    • Rotate weapons. Each item has its own cooldown and there is no global cooldown.
    • Pets deal a share of the enemy's max HP. That's strongest against high-HP enemies and bosses.

    Build types¶

    Build Focus Slots to prioritise
    Damage Highest weapon stat total + three damage accessories Weapon, Ears, Wrist, Tail
    Survival Highest-defence armour and tribe resist for your zone Hat, Body, Legs, Backpack (Backpack has the highest slot factor)
    Balanced (recommended while levelling) The best weapon available, then whichever slot gives the biggest stat jump Weapon first, then the weakest slot

    Skill points (Badge Book) can be reallocated and tribes switched for free, so you can respec for each zone's element.

    • Skill points come from levelling up (Badge Book). Every 5 points in a tribe give one badge level. Badge level n gives +75 + 20·(n−1) damage of that tribe's element and +67 + 24·(n−1) defence (confirmed in-game for levels 1–4: 75/67, 95/91, 115/115, 135/139). The tables in the data run to badge level 65 and the server has no 40-point cap. The damage bonus only helps weapons of that element. The defence bonus is base defence and counts against all elements (confirmed in-game: +139 to both Defence and Air defence at badge level 4). You can switch tribes for free. badge = floor(points / 5); dmg_bonus = 75 + 20·(badge−1); def_bonus = 67 + 24·(badge−1) Tip: Put skill points into the tribe whose element your best weapon has. Compare weapons including the bonus (the build calculator does this).
