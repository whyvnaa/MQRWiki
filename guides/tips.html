    Tips¶

    Each tip comes from a game rule on the mechanics pages.

    • Don't count on idol achievements for idols collected before level 3. Achievements only count progress from level 3. "(Daily)" achievements are not implemented yet, so they pay only once.
    • Don't judge buffs by the stats window. Resist and shield buffs work (Horn of Heroism: +250 all elemental resists for 25 s, 30 s cooldown; Potions of Shielding). The stats window does not show them. (player reports)
    • Reach checkpoints before risky fights. Being knocked out costs some of the bananas you picked up in the trail since your last checkpoint. Quest and selling bananas are safe. You wait 5 s to respawn, then get brief invincibility. Long buffs (e.g. Wisdom) survive death.
    • Wear armour of the enemy's tribe. It resists their element and still counts fully against blunt attacks. Armour stats are its scaled defence plus a resist line for tribe armour (e.g. Padded Ninja Hat: 51 defence + 18 Air resist; confirmed in-game). Against a given element, your defence = all defence + resist lines of that element. Against blunt (Crossroads) attackers, every resist line counts. The stats window's general "Defence" also includes all resist lines, and each element's defence counts only its own resist lines.
    • Stay at or above the recommended level of a trail. Being below a zone's recommended level gets you one-shot even with good defence (e.g. 340 defence, 1,400 HP in the Forbidden Keep arena).
    • To compare weapons, add each weapon's scaled_stats sum to your base. A higher sum always hits harder. A hit deals your base damage plus the scaled value of the weapon you are using. Base = ability power + all gear damage lines + skill-point bonuses. The weapon value is its scaled blunt + element (scaled_stats in items.json). There is no rounding surprise: the damage number equals the sum.
    • Wear the resist that matches the zone's tribe. Enemies attack with their tribe's element. The matching resist protects you: mist (Chim Foo/Shadow) enemies → Air resist. Tribes and elements: Bone=Earth, Outlaw=Fire, Shadow=Air, Wild=Ice, Grease=Lightning.
    • Put skill points into the tribe whose element your best weapon has. Compare weapons including the bonus (the build calculator does this). Skill points come from levelling up (Badge Book). Every 5 points in a tribe give one badge level. Badge level n gives +75 + 20·(n−1) damage of that tribe's element and +67 + 24·(n−1) defence (confirmed in-game for levels 1–4: 75/67, 95/91, 115/115, 135/139). The tables in the data run to badge level 65 and the server has no 40-point cap. The damage bonus only helps weapons of that element. The defence bonus is base defence and counts against all elements (confirmed in-game: +139 to both Defence and Air defence at badge level 4). You can switch tribes for free.
    • Prefer the highest item level you can equip. Ignore rarity unless it is Legendary or Unique. Item level (not the level requirement) drives stats. Rarity adds only about 1–4 % up to Epic; Legendary about +15–19 %; Unique about 2×. Items above level 65 drop to 1 (the stat tables end at 65).
    • Heal or jump right after loading into those trails. Known recurring bug: entering some trails (Mushroom Hollow ↔ Swamp of Misery, Lost Fortress, Bonsai Peaks …) can knock you out or take most of your HP. Healing may stay disabled until a real death or a restart.
    • Open fights against tough enemies with a stun (Stun Flower) and finish with your fastest weapon: a 4 s stun covers about 8 swings of a 0.5 s weapon. Use the build calculator's utility slot to compare roles. Weapon side effects work on MQReborn: Stun Flower stuns 4 s (cooldown 10 s) and Dragon Mace 1 s; Snake Staff (150 % over 10 s, plus its impact hit), Scorpion Sword (150 % over 30 s) and Rotten Stick (25 % over 5 s) poison; Turkey Mace (10 %), Flytrap Staff and Voodoo Doll (15 % in live data, 20 % in patch 1.7.5) steal life. Healing Staff heals 25 % of your HP every 20 s (self only); Horn of Heroism gives +250 elemental defence for 25 s every 30 s; Smoke Bomb gives 15 s of invisibility. A build is a melee weapon, a ranged weapon and one utility item chosen for its role, not for its damage. Not confirmed yet: whether poison is a % of the whole hit or only its earth part; whether Drop bombs (Stink Bombs) hurt enemies.
    • Fight enemies within ±4 levels of your own. Kill XP falls off when the enemy is 5 or more levels above OR below you, down to 1 XP. This is intended, to stop farming low zones. The exact curve is unknown.

    More¶

    • To compare weapons, use their stat totals on the item pages, not the inventory stat (it only shows your best hotbar weapon).
    • Buffs work even when the stats window doesn't show them.
    • Heal or jump right after loading into Mushroom Hollow, Swamp of Misery, Lost Fortress or Bonsai Peaks (known entry bug).
    • Collect idols only from level 3 on; achievements don't count earlier.
    • The Healing Staff only heals you. Deselect teammates before using it.
