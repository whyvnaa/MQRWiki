    Build progression by element¶

    For each slot, this guide lists every item that beats everything you could wear before, at the level you can first equip it. A great level-21 item shows up at level 21, not at the next round number. Pick the tab of the element you play: your Badge Book bonus only boosts weapons of your tribe's element, and element damage on accessories only counts for weapons of the same element.

    How to read the tables:

    • A build carries a melee weapon and a ranged weapon (thrown weapons, slingshots, blasters, staffs and damage zones), so each has its own rows. The weapon is the one the build calculator picks at that level: your hit with that level's best accessories, ranked by how fast it kills an enemy of your level. Score is your hit; Gain shows how many hits and seconds a kill takes, and what it took before.
    • Accessories (Ears, Wrist, Tail) score their blunt damage plus element damage of the tab's element.
    • Armour scores its defence plus resist against the tab's element. Armour of the enemy's tribe resists best (about 115 %), Crossroads armour is neutral (100 %) and other tribes' armour gives about 85 % against that element.
