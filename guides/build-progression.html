    Build progression¶

    This guide shows the best full set for every level from 1 to 60: which weapon, accessory and armour piece to wear from which level to which, and how to get it. There is one tab for each badge, and the Auto tab switches to whichever badge kills fastest at each level.

    Pick a tab, then read the timeline from left to right. Each bar is one item and spans the levels you wear it. Click a level to open it in the build calculator, where you see every alternative for each slot.

    How the sets are made:

    • All slots are picked together. Element damage on accessories only counts for weapons of the same element, so the accessories follow the weapons.
    • A set is rated by how fast it kills an enemy of your level and how much damage you take from the enemies in the zones around that level.
    • An item is only replaced when the new one is clearly better over the levels you keep it. A weapon that is a little stronger for one or two levels is left out.
    • Only items with a known way to get them are used. NickCash items are left out, and so is anything that takes more than about 30 hours to get. A few crafted items need materials from hundreds of days of daily boxes.
    • You get one skill point per level, and they all go into one badge.
    • The Stun and Heal rows are the best utility item to carry with that level's weapons.

    At every level the badge whose best set kills fastest. Changing your badge is free, so you can follow it: Shadow at levels 1–6, Outlaw at levels 7–19, Shadow at levels 20–60.
