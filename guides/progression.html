    Progression 1–60¶

    How to level fast¶

    1. Do quests first. They pay most of your XP. Idol-collector achievements (25,000 to 100,000 XP each) and the Warrior Games (10 rewarded runs per day, scaled to your level) come next.
    2. Fight enemies within 4 levels of your own. Kill XP falls toward 1 for enemies 5 or more levels away, above or below. In a group, everyone gets kill XP.
    3. Upgrade your weapon whenever you can. Your hit is your base damage plus the weapon's stat total, so a better weapon is the biggest single jump in power.
    4. Drink an Elixir of Wisdom (+20 %) before long quest or fighting sessions. It doesn't boost achievement XP.
    5. Don't enter zones above your level. Under-levelled monkeys get knocked out in one hit even with good defence.

    The level cap is 60. Each level adds +10 damage, +10 defence and +100 max HP, plus skill points for the Badge Book.
