    Progression 1–60¶

    How to level efficiently¶

    1. Quests first. They pay most of your XP; idol-collector achievements (25k–100k XP) and the Warrior Games (10 rewarded runs per day, scaled to your level) come next.
    2. Fight enemies within ±4 levels of your own. Kill XP falls toward 1 for enemies 5+ levels away, above or below. Group kills share XP.
    3. Upgrade your weapon whenever you can. Your hit damage is your base plus the weapon's stat total, so a better weapon is the biggest single jump in power.
    4. Use Elixirs of Wisdom (+20 %) during long quest or kill sessions. They don't boost achievement XP.
    5. Stay at or above a zone's level. Under-levelled players get one-shot even with good defence.

    The level cap is 60. Each level adds +10 ability power, +10 defence and +100 max HP, plus skill points for the Badge Book.

    • Kill XP falls off when the enemy is 5 or more levels above OR below you, down to 1 XP. This is intended, to stop farming low zones. The exact curve is unknown. Tip: Fight enemies within ±4 levels of your own.
    • Being below a zone's recommended level gets you one-shot even with good defence (e.g. 340 defence, 1,400 HP in the Forbidden Keep arena). Tip: Stay at or above the recommended level of a trail.

    Level bands¶

    Zones are listed by their difficulty, which is the base level of their enemies. Cities are left out. Quests are the highest-XP quests whose level requirement falls in the band. Weapons are the strongest you can obtain by the end of the band. Drop bombs are left out because they only damage breakables.
