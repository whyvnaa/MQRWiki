    Quartz Crystal¶

    A shiny chunk of quartz used for purification.

    How to get it¶

    Vendor

    • Muffin (Traveler in Rosy's Reef)
    • Hilia (Potions in Moss Temple)
    • Chinzee (Traveler in Stone Gardens)

    Chest / loot

    • The Crumbling Gates — up to 33.3 % per roll (17 loot objects)
    • Rosy's Reef — up to 17.4 % per roll (17 loot objects)
    • The Underground River — up to 17.4 % per roll (14 loot objects)
    • Stone Gardens — up to 17.0 % per roll (12 loot objects)
    • The Goon Cave — up to 15.5 % per roll (13 loot objects)
    • The Giant Tree — up to 15.5 % per roll (14 loot objects)
    • Forbidden Keep — up to 15.5 % per roll (16 loot objects)
    • Cindertop Highland — up to 10.0 % per roll (13 loot objects)
    • EnemyDrop_Generic — up to 2.5 % per roll (2 loot objects)
