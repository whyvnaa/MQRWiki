    Damage¶

    Every hit deals your base damage plus the stat total of the weapon you hit with.

    hit = base damage + weapon stat total

    Your base damage is the sum of:

    • 10 per level (your ability power),
    • the damage of your Ears, Wrist and Tail,
    • your Badge Book bonus (75 at badge level 1, +20 per further level), but only for weapons of that tribe's element.

    The weapon's stat total is on its item page: its blunt damage plus its element damage.

    Measured in-game

    A level 22 monkey with a base damage of 516:

    • Silver Stars (stat total 227) hit for 743.
    • Bo Staff (stat total 305) hit for 821.

    Blunt and element damage¶

    Weapons and accessories give blunt damage and, if they belong to a tribe, element damage. Blunt damage counts for every hit. Element damage only counts when you hit with a weapon of the same element.

    Example

    Brass Skull Earrings give 52 blunt damage and 20 Fire damage.

    • With Bo Staff, an Air weapon, they add 52 to each hit.
    • With a Fire weapon they add 72.

    Blunt makes up most of an item's damage, so the highest total usually wins even when the element doesn't match. Tribe items add element damage on top and are worth about 110 % of a Crossroads item of the same level (gear and stats).

    Comparing weapons¶

    A higher stat total always hits harder. Fast weapons make up for smaller hits: Bo Staff can hit every 0.5 s, Silver Stars every 0.8 s. The build calculator ranks weapons by how long they take to kill an enemy of your level, which accounts for both.

    The stats window shows one weapon

    The damage number in your inventory is your base plus your strongest hotbar weapon. Every other weapon hits for your base plus its own stat total.

    Weapon effects¶

    Some weapons stun, poison or heal on top of their hit. The bonus numbers in old patch notes, like "Silver Stars +50 Air", are the size of that effect and are not added to your hits.

    Item Effect
    Stun Flower stuns for 4 s, cooldown 10 s
    Dragon Mace stuns for 1 s
    Snake Staff poison: 150 % over 10 s, on top of its normal hit
    Scorpion Sword poison: 150 % over 30 s
    Rotten Stick poison: 25 % over 5 s
    Turkey Mace life steal 10 %
    Flytrap Staff, Voodoo Doll life steal 15 % (patch 1.7.5 said 20 %)
    Healing Staff heals you for 25 % of your max HP every 20 s, not your teammates
    Horn of Heroism +250 resist against every element for 25 s, every 30 s
    Smoke Bomb invisible for 15 s

    Poison is a share of your hit. Whether it's the whole hit or only the Earth part isn't confirmed yet.

    Tip

    Open tough fights with Stun Flower and follow with your fastest weapon. A 4 s stun covers about 8 swings of a 0.5 s weapon.

    Other damage¶

    • Gas clouds and other damage zones deal 50 % of your hit every tick. Their tooltip says a third, which is wrong.
    • Boom and TNT barrels only break with Fire weapons.
    • Super stomp deals (10 × level + 20) × 2, whatever your gear: 640 at level 30. (not confirmed on MQReborn yet)
    • Melee hits every enemy in front of you at once. Ranged shots fly straight for about 3 s and stop at walls. (not confirmed on MQReborn yet)
    • There are no critical hits, misses or dodges. A hit that seems to miss is lag. (not confirmed on MQReborn yet)
