    Welcome to the jungle¶

    A wiki for Monkey Quest on the MQReborn server, version 1.7.7. It explains how the game calculates damage, defence, XP and loot, where every item comes from, and what to equip at your level. All numbers come from the live game data of 2026-10-01.

    Build calculator Enter your level to get the best weapons, accessories and armour for every level. Guides Levelling from 1 to 60, upgrades per element, farming and tips. Mechanics Damage, defence, gear, XP, loot, buffs, pets and enemies. Items Every item with its stats and where to get it. Weapons All weapons with stat totals and cooldowns. Zones Enemy levels, loot, quests, vendors and the best armour for each zone. World map The in-game map with every zone and its level. Quests The quest journal of every tribe, dailies and all quests by level. Monsters Enemy types, how they attack and what they drop. Vendors Who sells what, and where. Tips Short, practical advice for fights, farming and levelling.

    Quick facts¶

    • The level cap is 60. Each level adds 10 damage, 10 defence and 100 HP.
    • A hit deals your base damage plus the weapon's stat total (damage).
    • Blunt damage counts for every weapon, whatever its element.
    • Kill XP falls toward 1 once an enemy is 5 or more levels above or below you (XP).
    • Everyone in a group gets the loot from enemies and chests (loot).

    About this wiki¶

    Item stats, drop chances, quests and zones come straight from MQReborn's game data. The damage, defence and Badge Book formulas were measured in-game. Statements marked not confirmed on MQReborn yet or player reports still need an in-game test. This is a fan project and not made by the MQReborn team.
