    Magma Citadel¶

    This ancient stronghold lies deep underground. Ka's strongest minions occupy this citadel.

    Type Tribe Difficulty (base enemy level)
    Trail Crossroads 54

    On the world map¶

    Open the Crossroads map

    Enemies here are around level 54 (plus a per-placement offset). Enemy HP at level 54: 5550, attack power: 2069. Kill XP is best within ±4 levels of your own (XP).

    Best armour against this zone (level 54)¶

    Enemies here attack with blunt damage. This is the highest defence per slot you can get by level 54 (known sources only). Full defence 2117 against enemy attack 2069 (you'd take only the minimum damage).

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence vs zone How to get
    Hat (Hair) Dread Plate Helmet Shadow 50 395 craft
    Body Dread Plate Armor Shadow 49 388 craft
    Legs Dread Plate Leg Armor Shadow 48 380 craft
