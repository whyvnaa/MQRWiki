    The Crumbling Gates¶

    At the edge of Ook stand these ancient gates. Built to prevent monsters from entering the land, they have since fallen into ruins.

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal blunt damage. The best armour you can get by level 40, scored by defence:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against blunt How to get
    Hat (Hair) Champion Warrior Helmet Crossroads 40 247 loot: EnemyDrop_Generic
    Body Champion Warrior Guardstraps Crossroads 40 247 loot: EnemyDrop_Generic
    Legs Champion Warrior Pants Crossroads 40 247 loot: EnemyDrop_Generic
    Backpack Veteran Explorer's Cutlass Outlaw 39 238 loot: Skull Cove

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 1379 defence against them. Their attack of 1528 then hits you for about 149. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
