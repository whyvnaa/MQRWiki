    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Air damage. The best armour you can get by level 48, scored by defence plus Air resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Air How to get
    Hat (Hair) Hardened Noggin Guard Crossroads 46 288 craft
    Body Shadow Fang Tunic Shadow 45 283 loot: Stone Gardens
    Legs Dread Plate Leg Armor Shadow 48 380 craft
    Backpack Dread Plate Shield Shadow 47 414 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 1845 defence against them. That is more than their attack of 1837, so they only deal their minimum damage. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
