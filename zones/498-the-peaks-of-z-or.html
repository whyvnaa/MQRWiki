    Map¶

    Every enemy, chest, portal and NPC in this zone, from the game's level data. Hover a marker for details, click it to open its page. The front and back paths are drawn separately.

    Drag to move · wheel or pinch to zoom · double-click to reset

    Enemies here are level 1 or a little higher. A level-1 enemy has 50 HP and 20 attack. You get full kill XP while your level is within 4 of theirs (XP).
