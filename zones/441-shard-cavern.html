    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal blunt damage. The best armour you can get by level 55, scored by defence:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against blunt How to get
    Hat (Hair) Dread Plate Helmet Shadow 50 395 craft
    Body Dread Plate Armor Shadow 49 388 craft
    Legs Dread Plate Leg Armor Shadow 48 380 craft
    Backpack Dread Plate Shield Shadow 47 414 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 2127 defence against them. That is more than their attack of 2108, so they only deal their minimum damage. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
