    Mountain Shrine¶

    A sanctuary in honor of the late Monkey King. Chim Foo students are often sent here to make a pilgrimage.

    Type Tribe Enemy level
    trail Shadow 9

    Enemies here are level 9 or a little higher. A level-9 enemy has 450 HP and 329 attack. You get full kill XP while your level is within 4 of theirs (XP).

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Air damage. The best armour you can get by level 9, scored by defence plus Air resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Air How to get
    Hat (Hair) Toucan Helmet of Power Crossroads 7 48 loot: The Volcano Lair
    Body Marsh Explorer Shirt Crossroads 9 45 craft
    Legs Marsh Explorer Pants Crossroads 9 45 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 228 defence against them. Their attack of 329 then hits you for about 101. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
