    Whispering Woods¶

    Buzzing with paranormal activity, this forested marsh is the perfect place for Ootu apprentices to connect with the spirits.

    Enemies here are level 5 or a little higher. A level-5 enemy has 250 HP and 174 attack. You get full kill XP while your level is within 4 of theirs (XP).

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal blunt damage. The best armour you can get by level 5, scored by defence:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against blunt How to get
    Hat (Hair) Blue Egg Hat Crossroads 5 38 loot: The Giant Tree
    Body Mystic Healing Garment Bone 5 12 loot: Whispering Woods
    Legs Leafy Jungle Kilt Crossroads 5 18 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 118 defence against them. Their attack of 174 then hits you for about 56. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
