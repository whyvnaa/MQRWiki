    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Fire damage. The best armour you can get by level 49, scored by defence plus Fire resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Fire How to get
    Hat (Hair) Sutler's Cap Outlaw 47 296 quest: PARTNER IN CRIME
    Body Boom Plate Armor Outlaw 49 388 craft
    Legs Boom Plate Leg Armor Outlaw 48 380 craft
    Backpack Boom Plate Shield Outlaw 47 414 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 1968 defence against them. That is more than their attack of 1876, so they only deal their minimum damage. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
