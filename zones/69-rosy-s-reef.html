    Rosy's Reef¶

    Rosy the Whale eats lots of things. Fish, ships, and precious treasures are on top of the list.

    Type Tribe Difficulty (base enemy level)
    Trail Outlaw 49

    On the world map¶

    Open the Sea Dragons map

    Enemies here are around level 49 (plus a per-placement offset). Enemy HP at level 49: 4718, attack power: 1876. Kill XP is best within ±4 levels of your own (XP).

    Best armour against this zone (level 49)¶

    Enemies here attack with Fire damage. This is the highest defence + Fire resist per slot you can get by level 49 (known sources only). Full defence 1968 against enemy attack 1876 (you'd take only the minimum damage).
