    The Chim Foo Rooftops¶

    Bounding across the Chim Foo rooftops is a quick way to traverse the trail but it takes grace and agility.

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Air damage. The best armour you can get by level 5, scored by defence plus Air resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Air How to get
    Hat (Hair) Blue Egg Hat Crossroads 5 38 loot: The Giant Tree
    Body Battered Ninja Tunic Shadow 5 12 loot: Hidden Hollow
    Legs Leafy Jungle Kilt Crossroads 5 18 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 118 defence against them. Their attack of 174 then hits you for about 56. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
