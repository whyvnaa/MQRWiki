    The Giant Tree¶

    In this spot lies the husk of Ook's tallest tree. Once a source of vitality for the entire Ootu region, its death caused the swamp to turn foul. To this day, nobody knows exactly why this ancient wonder perished.

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Earth damage. The best armour you can get by level 28, scored by defence plus Earth resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Earth How to get
    Hat (Hair) Crooked Burglar Cap Bone 27 144 craft
    Body Dastardly Shaman Suit Bone 28 132 craft
    Legs Belt of Ancestors Bone 28 129 loot: The Giant Tree
    Backpack Crag Scale Cloak Crossroads 24 150 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 835 defence against them. Their attack of 1064 then hits you for about 229. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
