    Anchor Falls¶

    A huge waterfall that drops into a deep canyon. At the bottom lives a Sea Dragon like no other.

    trail Outlaw 26

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Fire damage. The best armour you can get by level 26, scored by defence plus Fire resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Fire How to get
    Hat (Hair) Cap o' the Phoenix Outlaw 26 144 craft
    Body Jacket o' the Phoenix Outlaw 26 144 craft
    Legs Pants o' the Phoenix Outlaw 26 144 craft
    Backpack Crag Scale Cloak Crossroads 24 150 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 842 defence against them. Their attack of 986 then hits you for about 144. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
