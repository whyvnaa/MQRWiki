    Noogo Village¶

    A monkey village as peaceful as can be. Surrounded by fruit trees, the villagers may spend most of their time playing.

    Type Tribe Difficulty (base enemy level)
    TrailNoIdol Crossroads 1

    On the world map¶

    Open the Crossroads map

    Enemies here are around level 1 (plus a per-placement offset). Enemy HP at level 1: 50, attack power: 20. Kill XP is best within ±4 levels of your own (XP).

    Best armour against this zone (level 1)¶

    Enemies here attack with blunt damage. This is the highest defence per slot you can get by level 1 (known sources only). Full defence 12 against enemy attack 20 → about 8 damage per hit.

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence vs zone How to get
    Body Oak Plank Armor Crossroads 1 1 loot: The Forgotten Temple
    Legs Pale Skeleton Pants Crossroads 1 1 loot: Papa Matu's Haunted Graveyard

    For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator or build progression.
