    Hero's Secret Shrine¶

    This shrine is full of magma monsters. Chim Foo apprentices are sent there to master their stealth skills.

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal blunt damage. The best armour you can get by level 4, scored by defence:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against blunt How to get
    Hat (Hair) Leafy Jungle Hat Crossroads 3 11 craft
    Body Jungle Explorer Shirt Crossroads 4 7 loot: Whispering Woods
    Legs Oak Plank Pants Crossroads 3 11 buy: Xaken

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 69 defence against them. Their attack of 136 then hits you for about 67. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
