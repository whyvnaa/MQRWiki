    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Air damage. The best armour you can get by level 45, scored by defence plus Air resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Air How to get
    Hat (Hair) Shadow Fang Mask Shadow 45 283 loot: Stone Gardens
    Body Shadow Fang Tunic Shadow 45 283 loot: Stone Gardens
    Legs Shadow Fang Pants Shadow 45 283 loot: Stone Gardens
    Backpack Spring Ranger Cape Crossroads 45 302 buy: Pazza

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 1601 defence against them. Their attack of 1721 then hits you for about 120. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
