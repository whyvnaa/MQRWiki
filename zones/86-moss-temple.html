    Moss Temple¶

    An ancient Ootu temple abandoned for reasons yet unknown. According to the artifacts, some speculate that the inhabitants all mysteriously vanished at once. Surely, that's just a myth!

    trail Bone 46

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Earth damage. The best armour you can get by level 46, scored by defence plus Earth resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Earth How to get
    Hat (Hair) Hardened Noggin Guard Crossroads 46 288 craft
    Body Legendary Bog Hunter Shirt Bone 45 257 craft
    Legs Legendary Bog Hunter Belt Bone 45 257 craft
    Backpack Sickly Bat Wings Bone 45 308 buy: Etok

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 1570 defence against them. Their attack of 1760 then hits you for about 190. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
