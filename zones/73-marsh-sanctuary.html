    Marsh Sanctuary¶

    Ootu Mystics once collected mystic purified water from this refuge. With the return of the shadows, the water has grown foul and is unsuitable for potionmaking.

    Type Tribe Enemy level
    trail Bone 35

    On the world map¶

    Open the Ootu Mystics map

    Enemies here are level 35 or a little higher. A level-35 enemy has 2740 HP and 1334 attack. You get full kill XP while your level is within 4 of theirs (XP).

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Earth damage. The best armour you can get by level 35, scored by defence plus Earth resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Earth How to get
    Hat (Hair) Sun Knight Helmet Crossroads 35 206 craft
    Body Sun Knight Chestplate Crossroads 35 206 craft
    Legs Sun Knight Leg Armor Crossroads 35 206 craft
    Backpack Rugged Earthen Cape Crossroads 29 177 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 1145 defence against them. Their attack of 1334 then hits you for about 189. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
