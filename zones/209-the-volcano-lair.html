    The Volcano Lair¶

    Within this volcano lives the largest gorilla in Ook. He eats marshmallows.

    Enemies here are level 8 or a little higher. A level-8 enemy has 400 HP and 290 attack. You get full kill XP while your level is within 4 of theirs (XP).

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal blunt damage. The best armour you can get by level 8, scored by defence:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against blunt How to get
    Hat (Hair) Toucan Helmet of Power Crossroads 7 48 loot: The Volcano Lair
    Body Chic Pirate Jacket Outlaw 8 26 buy: Clunk
    Legs Chic Pirate Pants Outlaw 8 26 buy: Clunk

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 180 defence against them. Their attack of 290 then hits you for about 110. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
