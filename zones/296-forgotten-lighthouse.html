    Forgotten Lighthouse¶

    A lighthouse so tall that its beacon can be seen all across the Coconut Isles. Still, it is risky business to reach it.

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Fire damage. The best armour you can get by level 5, scored by defence plus Fire resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Fire How to get
    Hat (Hair) Blue Egg Hat Crossroads 5 38 loot: The Giant Tree
    Body Treasure Hunter Shirt Outlaw 5 12 buy: Clunk
    Legs Leafy Jungle Kilt Crossroads 5 18 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 118 defence against them. Their attack of 174 then hits you for about 56. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
