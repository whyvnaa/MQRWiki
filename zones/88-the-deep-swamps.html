    The Deep Swamps¶

    It's easy to get lost within this dense marsh. While many monkeys dare to enter these swamps as a test of mettle, not all of them return.

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Earth damage. The best armour you can get by level 10, scored by defence plus Earth resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Earth How to get
    Hat (Hair) Fruitcake Battle Helmet Crossroads 10 51 craft
    Body Marsh Explorer Shirt Crossroads 9 45 craft
    Legs Marsh Explorer Pants Crossroads 9 45 craft
    Backpack Divine Angel Wings Crossroads 10 56 buy: Akami

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 297 defence against them. Their attack of 368 then hits you for about 71. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
