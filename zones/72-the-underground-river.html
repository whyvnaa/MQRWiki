    The Underground River¶

    A hidden stream that cuts deep below the Swamp of Misery. It teems with toxic gas and powerful monsters, but you'll find precious artifacts so long as you can hold your breath.

    Type Tribe Enemy level
    trail Bone 50

    On the world map¶

    Open the Ootu Mystics map

    Enemies here are level 50 or a little higher. A level-50 enemy has 4879 HP and 1914 attack. You get full kill XP while your level is within 4 of theirs (XP).

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Earth damage. The best armour you can get by level 50, scored by defence plus Earth resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Earth How to get
    Hat (Hair) Venom Plate Helmet Bone 50 395 craft
    Body Venom Plate Armor Bone 49 388 craft
    Legs Venom Plate Leg Armor Bone 48 380 craft
    Backpack Venom Plate Shield Bone 47 414 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 2077 defence against them. That is more than their attack of 1914, so they only deal their minimum damage. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
