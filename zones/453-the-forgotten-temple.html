    Enemies here are level 1 or a little higher. A level-1 enemy has 50 HP and 20 attack. You get full kill XP while your level is within 4 of theirs (XP).

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal blunt damage. The best armour you can get by level 1, scored by defence:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against blunt How to get
    Body Oak Plank Armor Crossroads 1 1 loot: The Forgotten Temple
    Legs Pale Skeleton Pants Crossroads 1 1 loot: Papa Matu's Haunted Graveyard

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 12 defence against them. Their attack of 20 then hits you for about 8. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
