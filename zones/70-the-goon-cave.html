    The Goon Cave¶

    A huge shipwreck trapped in a cave. It's full of riches that are closely guarded by Shadow Monsters.

    trail Outlaw 33

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Fire damage. The best armour you can get by level 33, scored by defence plus Fire resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Fire How to get
    Hat (Hair) Pirate Medic's Hair Outlaw 33 192 craft
    Body Bold Warrior Chestplate Crossroads 30 175 craft
    Legs Bold Warrior Pants Crossroads 30 175 craft
    Backpack Rugged Earthen Cape Crossroads 29 177 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 1049 defence against them. Their attack of 1257 then hits you for about 208. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
