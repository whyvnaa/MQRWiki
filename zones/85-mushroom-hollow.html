    Mushroom Hollow¶

    The conditions of this damp forest cause the mushrooms to grow to incredible sizes. Novice alchemists travel here to test their gathering skills, or to have fun bouncing on the mushroom caps.

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Earth damage. The best armour you can get by level 5, scored by defence plus Earth resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Earth How to get
    Hat (Hair) Blue Egg Hat Crossroads 5 38 loot: The Giant Tree
    Body Mystic Healing Garment Bone 5 12 loot: Whispering Woods
    Legs Leafy Jungle Kilt Crossroads 5 18 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 118 defence against them. Their attack of 174 then hits you for about 56. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
