    Enemies here are level 11 or a little higher. A level-11 enemy has 550 HP and 406 attack. You get full kill XP while your level is within 4 of theirs (XP).

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Ice damage. The best armour you can get by level 11, scored by defence plus Ice resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Ice How to get
    Hat (Hair) Fruitcake Battle Helmet Crossroads 10 51 craft
    Body Marsh Explorer Shirt Crossroads 9 45 craft
    Legs Marsh Explorer Pants Crossroads 9 45 craft
    Backpack Divine Angel Wings Crossroads 10 56 buy: Akami

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 307 defence against them. Their attack of 406 then hits you for about 99. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
