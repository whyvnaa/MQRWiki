    The Floating Isles¶

    These isles are chunks of rock hovering above Ook. Only the bold reach the highest point.

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal blunt damage. The best armour you can get by level 2, scored by defence:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against blunt How to get
    Body Oak Plank Armor Crossroads 1 1 loot: The Forgotten Temple
    Legs Pale Skeleton Pants Crossroads 1 1 loot: Papa Matu's Haunted Graveyard

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 22 defence against them. Their attack of 58 then hits you for about 36. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
