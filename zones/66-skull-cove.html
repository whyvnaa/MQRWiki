    A maze of underwater caverns where holding your breath can mean the difference between life and death.

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Fire damage. The best armour you can get by level 38, scored by defence plus Fire resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Fire How to get
    Hat (Hair) Sun Crusader Helmet Crossroads 38 223 craft
    Body Dire Pirate's Coat Outlaw 38 225 craft
    Legs Dire Pirate's Trousers Outlaw 38 225 craft
    Backpack Royal Cape Crossroads 38 193 loot: The Crumbling Gates

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 1246 defence against them. Their attack of 1450 then hits you for about 204. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
