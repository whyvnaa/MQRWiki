    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal blunt damage. The best armour you can get by level 24, scored by defence:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against blunt How to get
    Hat (Hair) Swamp Crusher Mask Bone 21 115 buy: Zeeta
    Body Stinging Dragonfish Shirt Crossroads 23 112 craft
    Legs Swamp Crusher Kilt Bone 23 115 craft
    Backpack Crag Scale Cloak Crossroads 24 150 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 732 defence against them. Their attack of 909 then hits you for about 177. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
