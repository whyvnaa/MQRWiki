    The Spider Cave¶

    A series of winding tunnels infested by a kingdom of rachnoids. While the rachnoid soldiers may be hostile to monkeys, their queen seems to see things differently.

    Type Tribe Enemy level
    trail Bone 16

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Earth damage. The best armour you can get by level 16, scored by defence plus Earth resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Earth How to get
    Hat (Hair) Jungle Hat of Healing Bone 15 69 craft
    Body Novice Knight Chestplate Crossroads 16 90 buy: Xaken
    Legs Novice Knight Leg Armor Crossroads 15 81 buy: Xaken
    Backpack Adventurer's Backpack Crossroads 15 80 buy: Dillon

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 480 defence against them. Their attack of 600 then hits you for about 120. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
