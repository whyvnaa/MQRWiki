    Soggy Creek¶

    Several monkeys live in huts scattered around the creek. Recently, a scary prowling visitor has driven them off.

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal blunt damage. The best armour you can get by level 12, scored by defence:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against blunt How to get
    Hat (Hair) Nest Raider Mask Crossroads 12 53 craft
    Body Nest Raider Vest Crossroads 12 53 craft
    Legs Nest Raider Pants Crossroads 12 53 craft
    Backpack Bee Wings Crossroads 12 63 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 342 defence against them. Their attack of 445 then hits you for about 103. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
