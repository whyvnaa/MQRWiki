    Forbidden Keep¶

    Once the pride of the Chim Foo tribe, this fortified castle is now infested with shadow creatures.

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Air damage. The best armour you can get by level 31, scored by defence plus Air resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Air How to get
    Hat (Hair) Bold Warrior Helmet Crossroads 30 175 craft
    Body Bold Warrior Chestplate Crossroads 30 175 craft
    Legs Bold Warrior Pants Crossroads 30 175 craft
    Backpack Rugged Earthen Cape Crossroads 29 177 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 1012 defence against them. Their attack of 1180 then hits you for about 168. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
