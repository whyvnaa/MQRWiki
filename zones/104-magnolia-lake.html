    Magnolia Lake¶

    The beauty of the magnolias is breathtaking. Unfortunately, those who get hypnotized by them become easy prey for the Bombirds.

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Air damage. The best armour you can get by level 15, scored by defence plus Air resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Air How to get
    Hat (Hair) Padded Ninja Hat Shadow 14 69 craft
    Body Wacky Reindeer Suit Crossroads 15 66 buy: Starshine
    Legs Novice Knight Leg Armor Crossroads 15 81 buy: Xaken
    Backpack Adventurer's Backpack Crossroads 15 80 buy: Dillon

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 446 defence against them. Their attack of 561 then hits you for about 115. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
