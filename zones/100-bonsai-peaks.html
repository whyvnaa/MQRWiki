    Bonsai Peaks¶

    These mountains are the home of the famous Great Foo Panda, a unique animal of formidable size and appetite.

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Air damage. The best armour you can get by level 32, scored by defence plus Air resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Air How to get
    Hat (Hair) Crown of The Elements Crossroads 32 184 quest: THE JOY OF MUSIC
    Body Bold Warrior Chestplate Crossroads 30 175 craft
    Legs Bold Warrior Pants Crossroads 30 175 craft
    Backpack Rugged Earthen Cape Crossroads 29 177 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 1031 defence against them. Their attack of 1218 then hits you for about 187. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
