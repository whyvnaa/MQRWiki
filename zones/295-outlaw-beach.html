    Outlaw Beach¶

    A sandy beach where Sea Dragons used to celebrate their plunder. It is now the home to a rebel pirate.

    trail Outlaw 14

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Fire damage. The best armour you can get by level 14, scored by defence plus Fire resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Fire How to get
    Hat (Hair) Hearty Toucan Helm Crossroads 13 66 craft
    Body Hearty Sheep Vest Crossroads 13 55 craft
    Legs Reinforced Plank Pants Crossroads 14 63 craft
    Backpack Bee Wings Crossroads 12 63 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 387 defence against them. Their attack of 522 then hits you for about 135. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
