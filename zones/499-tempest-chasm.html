    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Ice damage. The best armour you can get by level 13, scored by defence plus Ice resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Ice How to get
    Hat (Hair) Hearty Toucan Helm Crossroads 13 66 craft
    Body Hearty Sheep Vest Crossroads 13 55 craft
    Legs Nest Raider Pants Crossroads 12 53 craft
    Backpack Bee Wings Crossroads 12 63 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 367 defence against them. Their attack of 484 then hits you for about 117. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
