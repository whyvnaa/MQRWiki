    Lantern Ridge¶

    Huge Chim Foo lanterns hang everywhere around this sacred land. Some say that one of them holds magical powers.

    trail Shadow 22

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Air damage. The best armour you can get by level 22, scored by defence plus Air resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Air How to get
    Hat (Hair) Reinforced Straw Hat Shadow 22 110 craft
    Body Forest Song Tunic Shadow 22 110 craft
    Legs Forest Song Pants Shadow 22 110 craft
    Backpack Fiery Skateboard Crossroads 20 108 buy: Scatterfoot

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 658 defence against them. Their attack of 832 then hits you for about 174. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
