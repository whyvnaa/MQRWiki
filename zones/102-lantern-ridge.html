    Lantern Ridge¶

    Huge Chim Foo lanterns hang everywhere around this sacred land. Some say that one of them holds magical powers.

    Type Tribe Difficulty (base enemy level)
    Trail Shadow 22

    On the world map¶

    Open the Chim Foo map

    Enemies here are around level 22 (plus a per-placement offset). Enemy HP at level 22: 1367, attack power: 832. Kill XP is best within ±4 levels of your own (XP).

    Best armour against this zone (level 22)¶

    Enemies here attack with Air damage. This is the highest defence + Air resist per slot you can get by level 22 (known sources only). Full defence 658 against enemy attack 832 → about 174 damage per hit.
