    World map¶

    The in-game world map: the World of Ook overview and the map of each tribe region, with the game's own art and layout. On a region map the trail connects every place, and its exit (the globe) leads to the highways. On the Ook map the highways link the regions, and the crests mark where each region's trail joins a highway. Hover a place for its level, click it to open the zone, and click a map, a tab, a crest or the globe to switch maps.
