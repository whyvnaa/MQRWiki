    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal blunt damage. The best armour you can get by level 45, scored by defence:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against blunt How to get
    Hat (Hair) Shadow Fang Mask Shadow 45 283 loot: Stone Gardens
    Body Shadow Fang Tunic Shadow 45 283 loot: Stone Gardens
    Legs Shadow Fang Pants Shadow 45 283 loot: Stone Gardens
    Backpack Sickly Bat Wings Bone 45 308 buy: Etok

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 1607 defence against them. Their attack of 1721 then hits you for about 114. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
