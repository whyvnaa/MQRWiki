    Sunken City¶

    Deep under the ocean surface stand the ruins of an ancient city. Courageous Sea Dragons search for lost treasure here.

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal Fire damage. The best armour you can get by level 44, scored by defence plus Fire resist:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against Fire How to get
    Hat (Hair) Blind Rage Bandana Outlaw 41 272 craft
    Body Champion Warrior Guardstraps Crossroads 40 247 loot: EnemyDrop_Generic
    Legs Champion Warrior Pants Crossroads 40 247 loot: EnemyDrop_Generic
    Backpack Forest Strider Cape Crossroads 44 240 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 1446 defence against them. Their attack of 1682 then hits you for about 236. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
