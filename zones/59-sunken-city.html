    Sunken City¶

    Deep under the ocean surface stand the ruins of an ancient city. Courageous Sea Dragons search for lost treasure here.

    Type Tribe Difficulty (base enemy level)
    Trail Outlaw 44

    On the world map¶

    Open the Sea Dragons map

    Enemies here are around level 44 (plus a per-placement offset). Enemy HP at level 44: 3952, attack power: 1682. Kill XP is best within ±4 levels of your own (XP).

    Best armour against this zone (level 44)¶

    Enemies here attack with Fire damage. This is the highest defence + Fire resist per slot you can get by level 44 (known sources only). Full defence 1446 against enemy attack 1682 → about 236 damage per hit.
