    Cindertop Highland¶

    A sundered wasteland crossed by rivers of lava. Many monkeys that have dared to enter have never returned.

    Type Tribe Enemy level
    trail Crossroads 48

    On the world map¶

    Open the Crossroads map

    Enemies here are level 48 or a little higher. A level-48 enemy has 4560 HP and 1837 attack. You get full kill XP while your level is within 4 of theirs (XP).

    Best armour for this zone¶

    Enemies here deal blunt damage. The best armour you can get by level 48, scored by defence:

    Slot Item Tribe Level req. Defence against blunt How to get
    Hat (Hair) Sutler's Cap Outlaw 47 296 quest: PARTNER IN CRIME
    Body Shadow Fang Tunic Shadow 45 283 loot: Stone Gardens
    Legs Dread Plate Leg Armor Shadow 48 380 craft
    Backpack Dread Plate Shield Shadow 47 414 craft

    With all four pieces and your base defence you have 1853 defence against them. That is more than their attack of 1837, so they only deal their minimum damage. For weapons and other levels, see the build calculator.
